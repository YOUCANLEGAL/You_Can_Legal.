🇵🇱 Poland Work Opportunity

Warehouse Worker (Magazynier)

Position & Industry

  • Logistics / warehousing — clothing, shoes, household goods and e-commerce
  • Exact city is assigned after arrival and onboarding

Job Responsibilities

  • Order picking with a scanner
  • Packing and preparing shipments
  • Receiving and sorting goods
  • Quality control
  • Removing security tags when required
  • Training is provided; Polish language and previous experience are not required

Working Schedule

  • 4–6 days per week
  • 8–12 hours per shift
  • Average 200–280 hours per month, depending on workload

Salary & Bonuses

  • 24.50–25.00 PLN net/hour
  • Students under 26: up to 30.50 PLN net/hour
  • Average monthly income: 4,500–7,500+ PLN net
  • Bonuses: up to 1,000 PLN
  • Monthly salary; an advance is available after the first working week

Accommodation

  • Employer-provided housing, 3–4 people per room
  • Approximately 550 PLN/month deducted from salary
  • Equipped apartments
  • Private housing may be partially or fully compensated depending on company policy

Contract & Support

  • Umowa Zlecenia
  • Assistance with Work Permit Type A
  • Assistance with Karta Pobytu
  • NFZ medical insurance support

Requirements

  • Age 18–55
  • Good physical condition
  • Willingness to work and learn
  • No criminal record

Employment Term

  • Minimum 1 year
  • Long-term employment available
  • Work can start shortly after arrival and onboarding

🇷🇸 Serbia Work Opportunity

General Workers & Skilled Positions

Available Positions

  • General workers: warehouse, loading/unloading, cleaning and manual labor
  • Possible skilled positions after arrival, subject to availability and requirements: construction workers, plumbers, carpenters, machine operators, drivers and agricultural workers

Salary & Schedule

  • €1,100/month
  • 6 days per week
  • 8–12 hours per shift
  • Approximately 200–280 hours/month depending on warehouse workload
  • Overtime may be available and compensated according to company policy

Accommodation & Transport

  • Dormitory accommodation: 2–4 people per apartment
  • Fully equipped accommodation
  • €150/month deducted from salary
  • Transport to and from work is provided

Benefits

  • 1 meal per day
  • Tax registration
  • Health insurance

What Is Not Covered

  • Flights
  • Personal expenses
  • Document-related costs

Document Preparation

  • Normally approximately 4–6 weeks
  • Invitation Letter
  • Employment Contract
  • Accommodation Confirmation Letter
  • Work visa Type D and Residence Permit application support documents
  • PPZ (Work Authorization from Serbian government)
  • Company Registration Documents

Translation Requirements

  • Documents are first translated in the home country into English or French
  • Documents are then translated in Serbia into Serbian

Additional Local Costs

  • Visa application fee: €35 online
  • Translation: approximately €15/page
  • Police clearance with translation

Application Process

  1. Sign the Service Agreement
  2. Make the required payment
  3. Paperwork is prepared, up to approximately 10 days
  4. Employer package is sent electronically
  5. Employer confirmation is obtained
  6. Online application is submitted to Serbian authorities
  7. Visa decision follows the applicable authority timeline
  8. Travel to Serbia; airport pickup is arranged
  9. Employer assists with the residence card process

Program Cost & Start

  • Service cost: €1,250
  • Start by providing a clear, high-quality scan of your passport with all pages in the correct order

🇸🇰 Slovakia Work Opportunity

Production Line Worker / Packing Operator

Positions & Salary

  • Production Line Worker; Packing Operator
  • €900–€1,600/month
  • Age limit: 55

Insurance & Accommodation

  • Health insurance and social contributions are paid by the employer (approximately €380) and are not deducted from salary
  • Dormitory accommodation: approximately €80–€150/month, deducted from salary
  • Transport depends on the factory and place of residence

Factory Tests

  • Logic test involving assembly of figures
  • Hand motor-skills test

What Is Not Covered

  • Flights
  • Visa fee
  • Travel insurance

Document Preparation

  • Normally approximately 3–4 weeks
  • Work Permit
  • Invitation Letter
  • Preliminary Employment Contract
  • Accommodation Confirmation
  • Police Clearance Certificate — obtained by the applicant; recommended approximately 1.5 months before the embassy appointment

Additional Local Costs

  • Super-legalization: €30 for listed non-apostille countries
  • Visa fee: €90
  • Medical insurance for 6 months: €150
  • Translations: approximately €45–€55
  • At least €1,000 recommended on arrival for registration, police procedures and local translations

Application Process

  1. Choose a package and send passport + CV
  2. Attend a video meeting
  3. Sign the Service Agreement electronically
  4. Make the required payment
  5. Book the official Embassy Appointment
  6. Prepare the Employment Promise (Prísľub zamestnania)
  7. Employer documents are sent by DHL
  8. Apply at the Slovak Embassy in your place of residence; processing follows the applicable authority timeline
  9. Travel and start the Official Employment Contract
  10. Apply for the Residence Permit (Zamestnanie)

Residence Permit

  • Stated validity: 2 years
  • Plastic residence card: approximately 2–3 weeks
  • EU travel is subject to applicable immigration and Schengen rules

Program Cost & Start

  • Work contract + stated 2-year residence permit arrangement: €1,800
  • Start by providing a clear, high-quality passport scan with all pages in the correct order
